# TDT Powersteel

Public marketing site and admin for a Manila-based steel supplier. Terms below are the ones used in specs, tickets, and code for the public content area.

## Language

**Product Application**:
A product type presented as a clickable circle on the Product Applications map, paired with a short video showing how it is used.
_Avoid_: hotspot, map pin, marker, circle object

**Product Applications map**:
The single illustrated artwork that shows the TDT facility, on which Product Applications appear as circles.
_Avoid_: facility map, interactive map, island map

**Application video**:
The self-hosted MP4 attached to a Product Application and played in the map's modal.
_Avoid_: product video, demo, clip

**Map label**:
The text rendered beside a Product Application's circle, naming the product type.
_Avoid_: caption, tag, title

**Anchor point**:
The spot on the Product Applications map that a circle's leader line points at.
_Avoid_: target, destination, pin

**Leader line**:
The orange stroke drawn from a Product Application's circle to its anchor point.
_Avoid_: pointer, connector, callout line
